About Choba School

Building strong foundations for every child

Choba School is a private primary school serving the local community in Choba, Pangani District in the Tanga Region of Tanzania. The school is deeply rooted in the village and aims to give children a safe, inspiring, and well-structured environment for learning.

With support from sponsors from the United Kingdom, the school continues to grow and create opportunities for students who need guidance, encouragement, and a chance to succeed in life.

Our mission

To provide quality education, character development, and practical support that helps students thrive academically and socially.

Our Sponsor Network

Supporting young learners and future leaders

Choba School is sustained by donations and sponsorship from kind supporters from the United Kingdom. This support enables us to maintain quality education and provide meaningful opportunities for promising students.

We also support alumni from the school who successfully complete Standard Seven and achieve excellent results in secondary education, especially those who come from poor backgrounds and need encouragement to continue their journey.

Scholarship focus

  • Academic excellence after Standard Seven
  • Support for students from disadvantaged homes
  • Empowering the next generation through education
  • Partnership with sponsors and community leaders
